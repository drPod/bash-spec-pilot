Dependency: libc 0.2 only. Std fs covers data copy, symlinks, hard links, dir
recursion, and permission bits; libc is needed for the syscalls std does not
expose for cp's documented attribute/special-file behavior: lchown (ownership),
utimes (timestamp preservation), and mkfifo/mknod (recursive special-file
copy).

Covered: all three synopsis forms (SOURCE DEST / SOURCE... DIR / -t DIR), -t/-T,
-R/-r/--recursive, -f/--force, -i/--interactive, -n/--no-clobber, --update
(all/none/none-fail/older) and -u, -v/--verbose/--debug, -l/--link,
-s/--symbolic-link, -L/-P/-H/-d/--dereference family, -p/--preserve/--no-preserve
(mode,ownership,timestamps,links), -a/--archive, -b/--backup[=CONTROL]/-S/--suffix
with VERSION_CONTROL + SIMPLE_BACKUP_SUFFIX, --parents, --strip-trailing-slashes,
--remove-destination, --attributes-only, -x/--one-file-system, --help, --version,
same-file detection, hard-link preservation across sources.

Skipped per harness contract: SELinux -Z/--context, NLS/locale, xattr/ACL
(accepted in --preserve lists but not applied), sparse/--sparse/--reflink
(accepted, plain copy performed), --copy-contents/--keep-directory-symlink
(accepted, no-op), signal handling, network/remote.
